How it works

From photos to 3D book in three steps

No account needed. No cloud storage. Everything runs right in your browser.

1

Design your pages

Open the creator and load a 3D book model. For each page, open the collage editor to add photos, resize and rotate them, set a background color, and layer elements exactly how you want.

2

Export as ZIP

When your book is ready, hit Export. Flipster packages every page image, the collage layout data, and the 3D model into a single portable ZIP file you can save or share.

3

Flip through it

Drop the ZIP into the viewer. Your scrapbook loads as a real 3D book you can rotate, pan, and flip page by page. Toggle pages on or off and export again whenever you want.

Features

Everything you need to build a scrapbook

A full creative toolkit, from image placement to 3D rendering.

Collage Editor

Drag, resize, rotate, and layer images on a fixed-ratio canvas. Shift-drag for proportional scaling. Shift-rotate to snap to 15-degree increments.

Page Management

Reorder pages by dragging, toggle visibility per page, and apply custom images or collages to any page in the book.

3D Book Viewer

Open exported scrapbooks in a WebGL-powered viewer. Flip pages with click or drag, pan the camera, and zoom in and out.

Undo & Redo

Full history support in the editor with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y. Never worry about making a mistake while designing.

Self-Contained Exports

Every export includes the 3D model, all page textures, and layout metadata in one ZIP. No external dependencies, no broken links. Share the file and it just works on any device with the viewer.
